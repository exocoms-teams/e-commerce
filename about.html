The Brand Architecture

Redefining corporate consumer chemistry through absolute structural transparency.

The Origin Sequence

Formulated on absolute biological purity.

LUMIÈRE was founded in response to the hyper-congested, mathematically complex matrices characterizing traditional consumer cosmetics. We believe luxury shouldn't rely on synthetic fillers.

Our research lab treats skin not as a temporary canvas, but as a living biological ecosystem that requires cellular compatibility to thrive.

The Extraction Standards

Botanical synergy backed by science.

Every extract used in our catalog is cold-pressed to preserve cellular integrity. We blend these potent extracts with proven clinical actives like stabilized Vitamin C, retinol, and ceramides.

This approach produces high-performance skincare that supports and strengthens your skin's natural barrier.

Our Foundations

The core operating values integrated into every chemical batch we produce.

100% Cruelty Free

We do not verify compounds or purchase raw ingredients from networks utilizing animal vectors at any stage of engineering.

Radical Transparency

We print our full ingredient weight values directly on our minimalist outer cartons. No hidden fragrance complexes or chemical loopholes.
